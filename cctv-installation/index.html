Cameras

Right camera for every location

We are not tied to one camera type. During the survey we select the camera, lens, mounting position and power/network method for each location — and reuse compatible cameras you already own where that is the practical choice. Everything then connects to the Parvekshak Security Appliance for recording, AI, alerts, playback and reports.

Fixed IP Cameras

Where
Entrances, corridors, rooms, shop floors and perimeter runs.
Why
Everyday coverage of a known, fixed field of view — the backbone of most deployments.
Connects
PoE over a single cable into the network switch and on to the appliance.

PTZ Cameras

Where
Large yards, campuses, parking areas and open plant land.
Why
One camera covers a wide area, with pan, tilt and zoom for detail on demand.
Connects
PoE or dedicated power, streaming to the appliance over the same network.

Varifocal Cameras

Where
Locations where the exact angle and reach must be set on site.
Why
The focal length is adjusted during installation for precise coverage of a gate, aisle or approach.
Connects
PoE into the switch, tuned at commissioning and left fixed.

Wi-Fi Cameras

Where
Spots where running Ethernet is impractical — retrofits, rented premises, outbuildings.
Why
Coverage without new cabling, where a wireless link is appropriate and stable.
Connects
Over your Wi-Fi network to the appliance; local power at the camera.

Low-Light / Night Cameras

Where
Perimeters, driveways, yards and any area with difficult lighting.
Why
Usable images in low light, which is when most intrusion detection matters.
Connects
PoE into the switch, with lighting and lux checked during the survey.

Specialised Cameras

Where
Gates and parking where vehicle or number-plate applications are required.
Why
Specialised requirements such as ANPR, subject to compatibility and project scope.
Connects
Into the same network and appliance, scoped per project.

About the appliance
Capabilities

AI capabilities you can switch on

Capabilities are selected per site during the survey. A home needs a handful; a plant may use security, safety and operations together. Nothing here requires new hardware on the wall once the cameras are in.

Security

Who is on site, where they are, and whether they should be there.
Intruder / Unknown Person Detection +
Detects
A person whose face is not on your enrolled list of family, staff or contractors.
Where it helps
Perimeter, entrances, driveways, yards, store rooms and staff-only floors.
Example
Someone climbs the boundary wall at 2:50 AM and walks toward the house.
Configurable response
Mobile alert with snapshot and clip · voice warning · floodlight · siren where installed.
Known-Person Recognition +
Detects
Enrolled family members, staff and contractors, so routine movement does not raise alarms.
Where it helps
Main gate, reception, lobby and entry cameras.
Example
Your housekeeper arrives at 9 AM and is recognised instead of flagged as an intruder.

Silent log entry · named notification where you want one.
Unauthorised Access +

A known person in a place or at a time they are not permitted.
Where it helps
Cash offices, server and records rooms, pharmacy and inventory stores.

Physical Response

Detection is only the first step

An alert on a phone is not much use at 3 AM if nothing happens on site. Parvekshak can trigger the response the premises allows — a voice warning in that area, the light, the siren — and keep the evidence either way.

  3. Local response Voice · light · siren
    →
  4. Security notified Mobile alert
    →
  5. Evidence stored Clip on site

Speaker / Voice Warning

A local warning in the area that was breached — challenging the person where they are, not across the whole site.

Siren / Hooter

Audible escalation where the site permits it, linked to confirmed events rather than every movement.

Floodlight / Light

The area lights up as configured, deterring the person and giving the camera a usable image.

Mobile Alert

A notification to the security desk, owner or management with the frame that triggered it.

Evidence Clip

The video around the event, kept on the appliance for verification and follow-up.

Reports

Compliance, attendance, counting and operational records available from the console.

Response devices are installed and configured per site. Sirens and floodlights are used only where the premises permits them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply the cameras, or do I buy them?

Either. We usually supply and install the cameras selected during the survey, but we also install customer-supplied cameras and regularly integrate compatible cameras that are already on the wall.

How long does an installation take?

A typical home or small office job of four to eight cameras is completed in a day, including cabling, configuration and a walkthrough of the app. Larger industrial sites are phased and scheduled around your operations.

Is the AI extra, or part of the installation?

AI security is part of the Parvekshak deployment, not a later upgrade. Which capabilities are enabled depends on the site — a home needs intruder and perimeter detection; a factory may also need PPE, fire and counting.

What about recording? Do I need an NVR as well?

No. The Parvekshak Security Appliance records and plays back the camera streams alongside the AI, alerts and management. Camera count, retention and storage are specified per configuration.

Can you upgrade an existing CCTV system instead of replacing it?

Often yes. Compatible IP/RTSP cameras in serviceable condition can be connected to the appliance and gain AI detection, alerts and reporting. The survey tells you what can be kept and what is worth replacing.

Do you handle service after installation?

Yes — commissioning, issue resolution, health checks, software updates and annual maintenance contracts. Support is part of the deployment.
