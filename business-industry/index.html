Restricted Zones & Access

A camera records the room. A zone watches it.

Your store rooms, server or records room, plant rooms and entrances are already on camera. What is missing is a rule: which area, during which hours, and who is allowed in it. Parvekshak marks each one as a zone and alerts the security desk the moment it is broken — not three weeks later when someone reviews the footage.

Rule part 01 Area

A polygon drawn on the camera view — the racks or the cabinet, not the whole frame.

+
Rule part 02 Permitted hours

Working hours, night only, or always.

+
Rule part 03 Who is allowed

Enrolled staff only, a named list, or nobody after hours.

=
Result Named alert

Security desk, in seconds, with the clip — and the area can answer back with voice and light.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to replace our existing CCTV?

Not necessarily. Compatible IP/RTSP cameras in serviceable condition can be connected to the Parvekshak appliance and gain AI, alerts and reporting. The survey identifies what is worth keeping, what needs re-aiming and what should be replaced for the coverage you need.

Which capabilities will actually be enabled on our site?

The ones your premises needs. A warehouse may run perimeter, restricted zones, box counting and attendance; a hospital may run watchlists, staff-only rooms, fall detection and crowd. Capabilities are configured during commissioning and can be added later.

Does fire detection replace our fire alarm system?

No. It is an additional visual detection layer that watches for visible flame and smoke in the camera view, which can matter in high ceilings, open yards, plant rooms and parking where heat and smoke take time to reach a detector. It works alongside your fire panel, not instead of it.

Where does the data live?

On the appliance inside your premises. Faces, clips and reports stay on site, and users and roles decide who sees which cameras.

Can alerts trigger a siren or a public-address warning?

Yes, where those devices are installed and the site permits them. A common configuration is a local voice warning in the breached area, the light in that area, and a silent alert plus clip to the security desk.

What does an industrial deployment cost?

Complete business and industrial deployments typically start from ₹2,00,000 and scale with camera count, site size, cabling and network, storage, the capabilities enabled and the response devices required. We quote after a site assessment.
