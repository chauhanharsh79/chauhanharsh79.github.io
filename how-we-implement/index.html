How We Implement

From first site walk to annual service

We don’t just install cameras — we build the complete security system. Every deployment follows the same sequence, whether it is six cameras at a home or sixty across a plant.

The Sequence

Eleven steps, in order

  1. 01

    Site Survey & Security Assessment

    We walk the site with your team and map risks, coverage, blind spots, lighting and the infrastructure already in place.

  2. 02

    Camera Placement & Coverage Design

    Camera locations, zones, network routes, storage and response points are designed before anything is bought.

  3. 03

    Camera Selection or Existing-CCTV Integration

    We select the camera type and lens per location, or integrate compatible cameras you already own.

  4. 04

    Cabling, PoE & Networking

    Cabling, PoE switches, power and mounting, run and terminated cleanly so the system stays serviceable.

  5. 05

    Recording & Storage Configuration

    Recording and retention are configured on the appliance for the camera count and the period you need.

  6. 06

    Parvekshak Appliance Installation

    The Security Appliance is installed on site and every camera is brought onto it.

  7. 07

    AI Capability Configuration

    Zones, rules, permitted hours, enrolled people, schedules and the capabilities your site actually needs.

  8. 08

    Response Integration

    Speakers, sirens, lights and floodlights wired and linked to the right events, where required.

  9. 09

    Testing & Commissioning

    Cameras, recording, AI, alerts and every configured response are tested end to end before handover.

  10. 10

    Training & Handover

    Your security and admin users are trained on the console, the mobile app and what each alert means.

  11. 11

    AMC & Support

    Ongoing service: health checks, issue resolution, software updates and maintenance under AMC.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a deployment take?

A home or small office of four to eight cameras is typically surveyed, installed and commissioned within days. Larger industrial sites are phased around your operations and scheduled at design stage, so production is not interrupted.

Do you quote before or after the survey?

After. The survey establishes camera count and type, cable routes, network and power, storage, and which capabilities are worth enabling. Quoting before that means guessing, and the guess is always wrong in one direction.

Can the installation be phased?

Yes. A common approach is to secure the perimeter and entries first, then extend to interior zones, safety and operations capabilities once the core is running.

Who configures the AI rules?

We do, with your team, during configuration and commissioning — zones marked on the actual camera views, permitted hours set to your shifts, and the people who need enrolling enrolled. You are trained to adjust them afterwards.

What happens at handover?

Every camera, the recording, each configured capability and each response device is tested in front of you, your users are trained on the console and app, and the support and AMC route is confirmed.
