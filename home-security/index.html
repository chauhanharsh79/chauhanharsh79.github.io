What It Watches For

Protect family, property and perimeter

Intruder & Unknown-Person Alerts

Every face is checked against the family, staff and regular visitors you have enrolled. Someone who is not on that list — at the gate after hours, in the driveway, on the terrace — is flagged the moment they appear, with the frame and clip.

Known-Family Recognition

The people who belong there are recognised, so routine comings and goings do not fill your phone with alerts. Recognition is configured for the cameras and people you choose.

After-Hours & Restricted Areas

A terrace, a store room, a rear lane, a boundary wall: mark the area, set the hours, and anyone there outside them raises an alert.

Perimeter & Night Monitoring

Boundary walls, rear gates, parking and service entries watched after dark, when it actually matters — with cameras chosen for the lighting you have.

Mobile Alerts with Image & Clip

A notification with the snapshot that triggered it and the video around the event, so you can tell a cat from a person before you get out of bed.

Loitering

Someone waiting beside the boundary for several minutes without approaching the gate is not a passer-by. Set the time, get the alert.

Capabilities are configured per home during the survey. Voice warning, floodlight and siren response require the matching devices to be installed.

Physical Response

Detection is only the first step

An alert on a phone is not much use at 3 AM if nothing happens on site. Parvekshak can trigger the response the premises allows — a voice warning in that area, the light, the siren — and keep the evidence either way.

Speaker / Voice Warning

A local warning in the area that was breached — challenging the person where they are, not across the whole site.

Siren / Hooter

Audible escalation where the site permits it, linked to confirmed events rather than every movement.

Floodlight / Light

The area lights up as configured, deterring the person and giving the camera a usable image.

Mobile Alert

A notification to the security desk, owner or management with the frame that triggered it.

Evidence Clip

The video around the event, kept on the appliance for verification and follow-up.

Reports

Compliance, attendance, counting and operational records available from the console.

Response devices are installed and configured per site. Sirens and floodlights are used only where the premises permits them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use the cameras I already have?

Often yes. Compatible IP/RTSP cameras in serviceable condition can connect to the Parvekshak appliance and gain AI detection, alerts and recording without being replaced. The survey confirms what can be kept.

Will I get an alert every time a cat walks past?

The system is tuned to people. Detection settings, zones and timings are adjusted during commissioning and can be refined afterwards — the aim is alerts you trust, not alerts you mute.

What happens at 3 AM when I am asleep?

Beyond the mobile alert, the system can act on site: a voice warning from the speaker in that area, the floodlight switching on, and a siren where it is installed and permitted. In a real Noida incident both intruders left within a minute.

Does my footage go to a cloud service?

No. Recording and detection run on the appliance inside your home. Internet is used only to deliver the alerts to your phone and for remote support if you want it.

What does a complete home system cost?

A typical complete home deployment is around ₹50,000 — cameras, recording, AI, installation and configuration together. The final figure depends on camera count and type, cabling, storage and any response devices. We quote after the survey.

Do you cover my area?

We install across Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad and Faridabad.
