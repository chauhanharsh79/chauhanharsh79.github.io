Frequently Asked Questions

Can Parvekshak work with my existing CCTV?

Yes. The Parvekshak AI Appliance adds intelligent AI detection to most existing IP/ONVIF cameras — no rip-and-replace needed.

Do I need internet or a cloud subscription?

No. All AI runs locally on the appliance, so there is no mandatory cloud and no monthly subscription. Internet is only used to deliver remote alerts if you want them.

How fast are the alerts?

Detection and alerting happen in real time — you get a mobile notification with a photo/clip within seconds of an event.

Can I add more cameras or AI features later?

Yes. Parvekshak is an expandable platform — add cameras and new AI modules anytime.

Do you provide installation?

Yes. We handle professional installation, upgrades, repair and AMC across Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad and Faridabad.
