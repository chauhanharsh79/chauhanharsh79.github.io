Cameras

Right camera for every location

We are not tied to one camera type. During the survey we select the camera, lens, mounting position and power/network method for each location — and reuse compatible cameras you already own where that is the practical choice. Everything then connects to the Parvekshak Security Appliance for recording, AI, alerts, playback and reports.

Fixed IP Cameras Everyday coverage of a known, fixed field of view — the backbone of most deployments.
PTZ Cameras One camera covers a wide area, with pan, tilt and zoom for detail on demand.
Varifocal Cameras The focal length is adjusted during installation for precise coverage of a gate, aisle or approach.
Wi-Fi Cameras Coverage without new cabling, where a wireless link is appropriate and stable.
Low-Light / Night Cameras Usable images in low light, which is when most intrusion detection matters.
Specialised Cameras Specialised requirements such as ANPR, subject to compatibility and project scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install the cameras, or only the AI?

Both. Parvekshak designs and installs the complete system — site survey, camera selection and placement, cabling, PoE and networking, recording and storage, the Parvekshak Security Appliance, AI configuration, response devices, commissioning and training. If you already have compatible CCTV, we can build on it instead.

Can Parvekshak work with my existing CCTV?

Where the cameras are compatible IP/RTSP units in serviceable condition, yes — they connect to the appliance and gain AI detection, alerts, recording and reporting without being replaced. We confirm what can be reused during the survey.

Is the Parvekshak appliance also the recorder?

Yes. The Security Appliance provides recording and playback alongside the AI, alerts and management, so you do not need a separate NVR. Camera count, retention and storage are specified per configuration.

Do I need internet or a cloud subscription?

No. Detection runs locally on the appliance inside your premises, and footage, faces and reports stay on site. Internet is used only to deliver remote alerts and remote support if you want them.

What happens after a detection?

The event raises a mobile alert with the frame and an evidence clip, and can trigger the response configured for that area — a local voice warning, a light or floodlight, and a siren or hooter where the site permits it.

Which areas do you cover?

Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad and Faridabad, for installation, upgrades, repair and AMC.
