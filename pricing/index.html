What Moves the Number

Why two sites with the same camera count cost differently

If your quote comes in above the bottom of a band, it will be for one of these reasons — and we will show you which.

Camera count and type

The single biggest factor. A varifocal or PTZ covering a yard costs more than a fixed camera on a corridor — and one well-placed camera often beats two badly placed ones.

Cable runs and network

Distance, routing, conduit and the number of PoE ports. A long run across a plant costs more than a short one inside a house.

Storage and retention

How many days you need to keep, at what resolution, on how many cameras.

AI capabilities enabled

A home running intruder and perimeter detection is not the same as a plant running PPE, fire, counting and attendance.

Response devices

Speakers, sirens, floodlights and the relay wiring to drive them, where the site permits them.

Existing CCTV reuse

Compatible cameras in good condition that we can keep reduce the number directly. The survey establishes what is worth keeping.

Power protection

UPS and power conditioning, which matters more on industrial sites than most quotes admit.

Site complexity

Height, access, working around production, and how much of the building is already wired.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the range a range, and where will my quote sit in it?

Because the same camera count can be a very different job. A four-camera home with short cable runs and an existing PoE switch sits at the bottom of the band; the same four cameras across a large plot, with a varifocal on the gate, a floodlight and a UPS, sits higher. The survey tells us which one you are, and the quote comes after it — inside the band, not above it.

What exactly is the platform cost shown inside each tier?

It is the Parvekshak Security Appliance and software — the recording, the on-premise AI, the app and the console. It is one line item within the complete system, not the price of a working installation. We show it so you can see what you are paying for, not as a starting price.

Can I use my existing cameras to reduce the cost?

Often, yes, and it can move the number meaningfully. Compatible IP/RTSP cameras in serviceable condition connect to the appliance and gain AI, alerts and recording. We run the compatibility check free during the survey and tell you plainly which cameras are worth keeping and which will not give usable footage whatever we do.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No mandatory cloud subscription — the AI runs locally on the appliance. The only ongoing cost is the AMC and support arrangement you choose.

Can I start small and expand later?

Yes, and it is a common approach: secure the perimeter and entry points first, then extend to interior zones and additional capabilities. Once the cameras and appliance are in, enabling more capabilities is a configuration change rather than new hardware, subject to appliance capacity.

Do you charge for the survey or the quote?

No. The site survey and the coverage design are free, and you get a written quote for the complete system before you commit to anything.
