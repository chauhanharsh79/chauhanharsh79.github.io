Frequently Asked Questions

What does the "Starting from" price include?

It covers the Parvekshak AI platform (appliance + software), installation support and a 1-year warranty. Cameras, storage and site-specific hardware are quoted separately based on your survey.

Do I need to buy new cameras?

Not necessarily. Parvekshak integrates with many existing compatible IP cameras, which can reduce your overall project cost. We do a free compatibility check during the survey.

Is there a monthly or cloud fee?

No. AI runs locally on the appliance — there is no mandatory cloud subscription.

How do I get an exact price?

Book a free site survey. We assess your property, check camera compatibility and give you a detailed, tailored quotation.

Need a Custom Solution?

Every property is different. We'll recommend the right AI package after a free site survey and provide a detailed quotation tailored to your requirements.
