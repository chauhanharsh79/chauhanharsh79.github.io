What Shapes a Quotation

Why we survey before we quote

These are the things that move the number. Any quotation that skips them is guessing, and the guess is always wrong in one direction.

Camera count and type

The single biggest factor. A varifocal or PTZ covering a yard is a different proposition to a fixed camera on a corridor.

Existing CCTV reuse

Compatible cameras in good condition that we can keep take the camera line out of the quotation entirely.

Cabling and network

Distance, routing, conduit and how many PoE ports the site needs.

Recording and retention

How many days you keep, at what resolution, across how many cameras.

AI capabilities enabled

A home running intruder and perimeter detection is not the same as a plant running PPE, fire, counting and attendance.

Response devices

Speakers, sirens, floodlights and the wiring to drive them, where the site permits them.

Power protection

UPS and conditioning, which matters more on industrial sites than most quotations admit.

Site complexity

Height, access, working around production, and how much of the building is already wired.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no price list on this page?

Because a single figure would be wrong for most sites. Two customers with the same camera count can differ by a wide margin once cabling runs, storage retention, response devices and how much existing CCTV can be reused are taken into account. We would rather survey the site and give you a number we can stand behind than publish one that changes at quotation stage.

What do I get at the end of the assessment?

A coverage design showing what each camera would see, the capabilities worth enabling for your premises, and a written quotation for the complete system — cameras, cabling, recording, AI, response and installation. No charge, and no obligation.

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. The site survey and the coverage design cost nothing. We would rather spend a couple of hours understanding the site than quote blind.

Can I use my existing cameras to reduce the cost?

Often, and it can change the number meaningfully. Compatible IP/RTSP cameras in serviceable condition connect to the appliance and gain AI detection, alerts and recording. The compatibility check is free and we will tell you plainly which cameras are worth keeping.

Is there a monthly subscription?

There is no mandatory cloud subscription for the AI to run — detection and recording are local to the appliance on your premises. Ongoing cost is the support and AMC arrangement you choose, scoped to your deployment.

Can we start small and expand later?

Yes, and it is a common approach: secure the perimeter and entry points first, then extend to interior zones and additional capabilities. Once the cameras and appliance are in, enabling more capabilities is a configuration change rather than new hardware, subject to appliance capacity.
