Why Maintenance Matters

Systems drift

A CCTV + AI deployment is not a one-off purchase that stays correct forever. The site changes around it, and detection quality follows camera condition, aim and lighting.

CCTV AMC in Noida Talk to us
  • Cameras drift out of aim, get knocked, or get obscured by a growing tree
  • Lighting changes — a new lamp, a removed one, a repainted wall
  • Sites change: a new store room, a relocated dispatch bay, a new shift pattern
  • Staff change, so enrolled people and permitted lists need maintaining
  • Storage fills differently once you know what you actually review
  • Cabling and connectors age, especially outdoors

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an AMC cover?

AMC scope is agreed per deployment and typically covers periodic health checks, preventive maintenance, issue resolution, software updates and service visits. It is scoped to the size and criticality of your system rather than sold as a single fixed package.

How quickly do you respond?

Response and resolution expectations are set in your AMC agreement rather than promised generically on a website. We would rather agree something specific with you and meet it.

Do you support systems you did not install?

Talk to us. It depends on the equipment, the condition of the cabling and whether the system can be brought onto the Parvekshak appliance sensibly. Sometimes the honest answer is that a partial rebuild is cheaper than maintaining what is there.

Is remote support possible?

Where the site has connectivity and you permit it, many issues can be diagnosed and resolved remotely, which is faster than waiting for a visit. Sites can also run entirely offline if you prefer.

What if we add cameras later?

New cameras are surveyed, installed and brought onto the existing appliance, subject to the configuration having capacity. That is part of what an AMC review looks at.

Already have a system that nobody maintains?

Tell us what is installed. We will assess the cameras, cabling and coverage, and tell you honestly what is worth keeping.
