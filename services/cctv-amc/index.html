CCTV Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) in Delhi NCR

CCTV only protects you if it is actually working on the day something happens. A Parvekshak Annual Maintenance Contract keeps your system healthy with scheduled inspections, cleaning and priority repairs, so you never discover a dead camera during an incident. It turns security from a one-time install into something you can rely on all year.

What You Get

CCTV AMC

Scheduled Health Checks

Regular visits verify that every camera, recorder and storage device is functioning, so problems are caught before they cost you footage.

Cleaning & Alignment

Lenses are cleaned and angles corrected over time, keeping images sharp and coverage accurate as conditions and mountings shift.

AMC customers get faster turnaround on faults, minimising the time any part of your premises is left without working coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the AMC include?

It covers scheduled inspections, lens cleaning and alignment, recorder and storage checks, and priority repair response, keeping your entire system dependable throughout the year.

Can I take an AMC on a system you didn't install?

Yes. We offer maintenance contracts for existing CCTV regardless of who installed it, after an initial assessment of the system's condition.

How often will technicians visit?

Visit frequency is set to suit your site and contract, typically periodic scheduled visits plus priority call-outs whenever a fault is reported.
