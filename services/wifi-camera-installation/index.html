Frequently Asked Questions

Are WiFi cameras reliable enough for security?

When installed properly with attention to signal strength and placement, wireless cameras are dependable. We survey your coverage and access points to ensure a stable connection at every camera.

Do WiFi cameras still need power cabling?

Yes. The video travels wirelessly, but each camera still needs a power source. We plan clean, short power runs so the install remains tidy.

Can I get AI alerts on wireless cameras?

Yes. Wireless cameras can feed Parvekshak's local AI, so you receive intrusion and face-detection alerts on your phone just as you would with wired cameras.
