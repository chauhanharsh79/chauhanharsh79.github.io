Frequently Asked Questions

Which camera brands do you support?

We support all major ONVIF-compatible camera brands and integrate with most existing CCTV systems.

Can you upgrade my existing cameras with AI?

Yes — in most cases the AI Box adds intelligent detection to your current ONVIF cameras without replacing them.

Do you provide the cameras, or just installation?

Both. We supply cameras and equipment or work with your existing hardware, then install and configure everything.

Do you handle cabling and NVR setup?

Yes — we deliver complete infrastructure: cabling, PoE, NVR/DVR setup, remote viewing and more.
