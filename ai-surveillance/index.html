Frequently Asked Questions

Does AI surveillance need the internet?

No. All analysis runs locally on the AI Box. Internet is only used to deliver remote alerts if you want them.

Is my video sent to the cloud?

No. Parvekshak is privacy-first — video is processed and stored locally, with no mandatory cloud upload.

Will it work with my current cameras?

In most cases yes. The AI Box supports most existing ONVIF-compatible CCTV cameras.

Can it reduce false alarms?

Yes. AI detection is far more accurate than basic motion alerts, focusing on people, vehicles and real events.
